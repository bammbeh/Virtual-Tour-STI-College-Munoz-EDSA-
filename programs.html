Our Programs

Explore the various academic tracks and courses we offer at STI College Muñoz-EDSA.

Senior High School Tracks

Be college-ready with the knowledge, training, and preparation to pursue a degree, start a business, or gain employment.

Science, Technology, Engineering, and Mathematics (STEM)
  • Core Subjects: Advanced Math, Physics, Chemistry
  • Specialized Subjects: Calculus, Biology, Chemistry, and Physics
  • Career Pathways: Engineering, Health Sciences, IT

Accountancy, Business, and Management (ABM)
  • Core Subjects: Business Math, Accounting
  • Specialized Subjects: Organization & Management, Business Mathematics, Principles of Marketing, Accountancy, Business, and Management, Finance, Economics, Business Ethics
  • Career Pathways: Accounting, Finance, Entrepreneurship

Humanities and Social Sciences (HUMSS)
  • Core Subjects: Communication, Philosophy
  • Specialized Subjects: Sociology, Psychology
  • Career Pathways: Education, Law, Journalism

Information Technology in Mobile App and Web Development (ITMAWD)
  • Core Subjects: Programming, Web Development
  • Specialized Subjects: Mobile App Development, UI/UX Design
  • Career Pathways: Software Development, Web Development, IT Support

Computer & Communications Technology (CCTEC)
  • Core Subjects: Networking, Digital Communication
  • Specialized Subjects: Network Security, Telecommunications
  • Career Pathways: IT Networking, Telecom Engineering, System Administration

Culinary Arts (CUARTS)
  • Core Subjects: Food Safety, Nutrition
  • Specialized Subjects: Culinary Techniques, Pastry Arts
  • Career Pathways: Chef, Restaurant Management, Food Styling

Digital Arts (DIGAR)
  • Core Subjects: Graphic Design, Photography
  • Specialized Subjects: 3D Animation, Video Editing
  • Career Pathways: Graphic Design, Multimedia Arts, Animation

Restaurant & Cafe Operations (RESCO)
  • Core Subjects: Hospitality, Food and Beverage Service
  • Specialized Subjects: Restaurant Management, Culinary Arts
  • Career Pathways: Restaurant Management, Café Operations, Catering

Tourism Operations (TOPER)
  • Core Subjects: Travel and Tourism, Hospitality
  • Specialized Subjects: Tour Guiding, Event Planning
  • Career Pathways: Tour Operations, Travel Agency, Event Management

Tertiary Courses

Be life-ready with the necessary skills and knowledge to be an in-demand professional.

Bachelor of Multimedia Art
  • Core Subjects: Digital Photography, Graphic Design
  • Specialized Subjects: 3D Animation, Video Production
  • Career Pathways: Multimedia Artist, Animator, Video Editor

Bachelor of Science in Computer Science
  • Core Subjects: Data Structures, Algorithms
  • Specialized Subjects: Software Engineering, Artificial Intelligence
  • Career Pathways: Software Developer, Systems Analyst

Bachelor of Science in Information Technology
  • Core Subjects: Programming, Networking
  • Specialized Subjects: Web Development, Database Management
  • Career Pathways: IT Specialist, Network Administrator

Bachelor of Science in Computer Engineering
  • Core Subjects: Circuit Analysis, Digital Logic
  • Specialized Subjects: Embedded Systems, Microprocessors
  • Career Pathways: Computer Engineer, Hardware Developer

Bachelor of Science in Hospitality Management
  • Core Subjects: Hospitality Management, Food and Beverage Service
  • Specialized Subjects: Hotel Operations, Event Planning
  • Career Pathways: Hotel Manager, Event Coordinator

Bachelor of Science in Tourism Management
  • Core Subjects: Tourism Planning, Marketing
  • Specialized Subjects: Tour Operations, Sustainable Tourism
  • Career Pathways: Travel Consultant, Tourism Officer

Bachelor of Science in Accounting Information System
  • Core Subjects: Financial Accounting, Database Systems
  • Specialized Subjects: AIS Systems, IT Auditing
  • Career Pathways: Accountant, IT Auditor

Bachelor of Arts in Psychology
  • Core Subjects: Introduction to Psychology, Human Development
  • Specialized Subjects: Clinical Psychology, Behavioral Science
  • Career Pathways: Psychologist, HR Specialist, Counselor
