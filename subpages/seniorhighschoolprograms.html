Our Senior High School Tracks

Be college-ready with the knowledge, training, and preparation to pursue a degree, start a business, or gain employment.

Science, Technology, Engineering, and Mathematics (STEM)
  • Core Subjects: Advanced Math, Physics, Chemistry
  • Specialized Subjects: Calculus, Biology, Chemistry, and Physics
  • Career Pathways: Engineering, Health Sciences, IT

Accountancy, Business, and Management (ABM)
  • Core Subjects: Business Math, Accounting
  • Specialized Subjects: Business Management, Marketing
  • Career Pathways: Accounting, Finance, Entrepreneurship

Humanities and Social Sciences (HUMSS)
  • Core Subjects: Communication, Philosophy
  • Specialized Subjects: Sociology, Psychology
  • Career Pathways: Education, Law, Journalism

Information Technology in Mobile App and Web Development (ITMAWD)
  • Core Subjects: Programming, Web Development
  • Specialized Subjects: Mobile App Development, UI/UX Design
  • Career Pathways: Software Development, Web Development, IT Support

Computer & Communications Technology (CCTEC)
  • Core Subjects: Networking, Digital Communication
  • Specialized Subjects: Network Security, Telecommunications
  • Career Pathways: IT Networking, Telecom Engineering, System Administration

Culinary Arts (CUARTS)
  • Core Subjects: Food Safety, Nutrition
  • Specialized Subjects: Culinary Techniques, Pastry Arts
  • Career Pathways: Chef, Restaurant Management, Food Styling

Digital Arts (DIGAR)
  • Core Subjects: Graphic Design, Photography
  • Specialized Subjects: 3D Animation, Video Editing
  • Career Pathways: Graphic Design, Multimedia Arts, Animation

Restaurant & Cafe Operations (RESCO)
  • Core Subjects: Hospitality, Food and Beverage Service
  • Specialized Subjects: Restaurant Management, Culinary Arts
  • Career Pathways: Restaurant Management, Café Operations, Catering

Tourism Operations (TOPER)
  • Core Subjects: Travel and Tourism, Hospitality
  • Specialized Subjects: Tour Guiding, Event Planning
  • Career Pathways: Tour Operations, Travel Agency, Event Management
