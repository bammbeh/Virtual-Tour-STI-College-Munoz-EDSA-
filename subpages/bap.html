Bachelor of Arts in Psychology

Explore Human Behavior and the Mind with BA Psychology.

The BA in Psychology program provides in-depth study of human behavior, thoughts, and emotions. The comprehensive curriculum covers key areas like clinical, social, developmental, and cognitive psychology. Students learn to understand psychological theories, research methods, ethics, human behavior patterns, social interactions, and mental health conditions.

This program prepares students for a career in teaching, research, and practice in the field of psychology. It also serves as a foundation for graduate studies in psychology or other fields such as law.

Top Skills to Learn

  • Psychological Theories
  • Scientific Reasoning
  • Ethics in Psychology Research
  • Research Methods in Psychology
  • Data Analysis and Interpretation
  • Understanding Human Behavior
  • Understanding Mental Health Conditions
  • Psychological Testing and Assessment
  • Human Resource Development
  • Introduction to Clinical Psychology

Admission Requirements

The following requirements must be submitted to the Registrar's Office before admission to any academic program:

College Freshmen

  • Original Form 138 / SF9-SHS (Learner's Progress Report Card)
  • Original Form 137 / SF10-SHS (Learner's Permanent Academic Record)
  • PSA-issued Birth Certificate
  • Original Copy of Certificate of Good Moral Character or Recommendation from the Dean/Program or Department Head
  • Medical Certificate of Chest X-ray results

College Transferees

  • Certificate of Transfer (Honorable Dismissal)
  • Official Transcript of Records
  • PSA-issued Birth Certificate
  • Original Copy of Certificate of Good Moral Character or Recommendation from the Dean/Program or Department Head
  • Medical Certificate of Chest X-ray results
