REQUIREMENTS

The following requirements must be submitted to the Registrar's Office before admission to any academic program:

  • Original Form 138 / SF9-ES (Learner's Progress Report Card)
  • Original Form 137 / SF10-ES (Learner's Permanent Academic Record)
  • PSA-issued Birth Certificate
  • Original Copy of Certificate of Good Moral Character or Recommendation from the School Principal
  • Medical Certificate of Chest X-ray results

Grade 11

  • Original Form 138/SF9-JHS (Learner’s Progress Report Card)
  • Original Form 137/SF10-JHS (Learner’s Permanent Academic Record)
  • PSA-issued Birth Certificate
  • Original Copy of Certificate of Good Moral Character or Recommendation from the School Principal
  • Medical Certificate of Chest X-ray results

Senior High School Transferees

  • Certificate of Transfer (Honorable Dismissal)
  • Original Form 138/SF9-SHS
  • Original Form 137/SF10-SHS (Copy for STI)
  • PSA-issued Birth Certificate
  • Original Copy of Certificate of Good Moral Character or Recommendation from the School Principal
  • Medical Certificate of Chest X-ray results

Freshmen

  • Original Form 138 / SF9-SHS (Learner's Progress Report Card)
  • Original Form 137 / SF10-SHS (Learner's Permanent Academic Record)
  • PSA-issued Birth Certificate
  • Original Copy of Certificate of Good Moral Character or Recommendation from the Principal
  • Medical Certificate of Chest X-ray results

Transferees

  • Certificate of Transfer (Honorable Dismissal)
  • Official Transcript of Records
  • PSA-issued Birth Certificate
  • Original Copy of Certificate of Good Moral Character or Recommendation from the Dean/Program or Department Head
  • Medical Certificate of Chest X-ray results
  • Certificate of Rating (COR) indicating eligibility
  • PSA-issued Birth Certificate
  • Clearances: Barangay, Police, or NBI
  • Certificate of Good Moral Character
  • Medical Certificate of Chest X-ray results

  • Authenticated Birth Certificate or Passport
  • Visa and permits (if applicable)
  • Certificate of Good Moral Character
  • Medical Certificate of Chest X-ray results

Enroll Online

  • Go to apply.sti.edu.
  • Pay the reservation fee online or through our partners.
  • Send your proof of payment to the campus’ official Facebook messenger.
  • Submit the necessary requirements before the start of classes.

Enroll Onsite

  • Visit www.sti.edu/campussafety.
  • Go to your preferred campus and fill out the application form.
  • Pay the indicated amount and get the official receipt.
  • Submit the necessary requirements before the start of classes.
