ORGANIZATIONS

At STI we are committed to fostering a dynamic and innovative environment that empowers individuals to reach their full potential.

As an organization, we value talent, dedication, and a shared passion for excellence. With a strong focus on providing quality education and professional development, STI is dedicated to shaping the future of our students and creating a lasting impact on the communities we serve. We are proud to be a forward-thinking institution with a mission to continually evolve and contribute to the success of every individual who joins our community.

Central Student Government

The Central Student Government (CSG), as the extension service of the college, is organized to develop creative, independent, responsible, and professional student leadership geared towards the development of the college and of the studentry, as well as of the society, and shall be the voice of the studentry for their legitimate needs, aspirations, and interests.

STI Alumni Association- Muñoz-EDSA

Our Local Alumni Chapter is the first in Metro Manila to be recognized by STIAA-Head Office. The Adviser of this association is Mr. Wilson Morano.
